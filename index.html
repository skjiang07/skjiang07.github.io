Mobility data · GeoAI · Resilience

Shangkun Jiang

Ph.D. candidate in Civil Engineering · University of Florida

I use mobility data to understand travel, traffic, and evacuation.

I am advised by Dr. Xilei Zhao and work as a research assistant at UF and the McTrans Center. My research focuses on mobility data quality, GeoAI, and transportation resilience.

My dissertation connects the practical use of emerging mobility data with the biases and limitations that shape its value.

Selected projects

GeoAI-Act: GPS activity inference2026 - present

A two-stage framework for stay detection and activity-purpose inference using Transformers and LLMs. Stay detection achieved 0.873 point-level F1 and 0.813 segment-level F1.

GPS trajectories · OpenStreetMap · Llama-3.1 + QLoRA

Spatial bias in mobility data2025 - present

Analyzed 10M+ anonymized mobile device users across Florida, identifying systematic underrepresentation in socially disadvantaged and urban areas.

Wildfire evacuation behavior2024 - 2025

Used Facebook data to estimate evacuation compliance, travel distances, and destinations during the 2025 Palisades and Eaton fires.

Read paper ↗
Evacuation orders & traffic2024

Estimated the traffic effects of Hurricane Ian evacuation orders: a 14.9% decrease in average speed and a 14.6% increase in travel-time variability.

Read paper ↗
Transit buses as traffic probes2023

Estimated network-level traffic speeds from GTFS realtime data, with validation against Bluetooth sensors and Google traffic data.

Read paper ↗
Inland vessel traffic flow2023

Developed a piecewise fundamental diagram from multi-source vessel data to describe speed, spacing, and density in restricted waterways.
