Profile

Research at the intersection of mobility data, transportation operations, and disaster resilience.

My dissertation, Advancing Transportation Systems Analysis with Emerging Mobility Data: From Operational Utility to Data Quality, examines how GPS, mobile device, transit, and social media data can support transportation planning and emergency management while accounting for bias and uncertainty.

I work across computational modeling, causal inference, machine learning, statistical analysis, travel behavior, urban traffic, evacuation, emergency traffic modeling, and transportation resilience.

Selected Projects

Current and recent research

University of Florida May 2026 - Present

GeoAI-Based Activity Inference from GPS Trajectories

Developed GeoAI-Act, a two-stage framework that converts noisy GPS trajectories into semantically labeled stay episodes using Transformer-based stay detection and LLM-based purpose inference.

  • Engineered trajectory, temporal, data-quality, and OSM-based geographic features.
  • Achieved 0.873 point-level F1 and 0.813 segment-level F1 for stay detection.
  • Improved stay purpose inference using Llama-3.1 with QLoRA.
University of Florida Sept. 2025 - Present

Assessing Spatial Bias of Mobile Device Location Data

Developed a framework to evaluate spatial bias in large-scale mobility datasets using more than 10 million anonymized mobile device users across Florida.

  • Identified systematic underrepresentation in socially disadvantaged and urban areas.
  • Assessed implications for transportation analysis and policy applications.
University of Florida Sept. 2024 - Feb. 2025

Wildfire Evacuation Behavior Using Facebook Data

Analyzed evacuation behavior during the 2025 Palisades and Eaton fires using Facebook mobility data.

  • Quantified evacuation compliance rates, travel distances, and destination types.
  • Identified spatial disparities across communities with different socioeconomic variables.
University of Florida May 2024 - Sept. 2024

Evacuation Orders and Traffic Conditions During Hurricane Ian

Estimated the short-term traffic impacts of evacuation orders using high-resolution mobility datasets and causal modeling.

  • Estimated space-mean speed and planning time index from mobile device location data.
  • Found a 14.9% decrease in average traffic speed and a 14.6% increase in travel time variability.
University of Florida Jul. 2023 - Dec. 2023

Real-Time Urban Traffic Monitoring Using Transit Buses as Probes

Developed algorithms to estimate network-level traffic speeds using GTFS realtime transit data.

  • Designed bus segment-trip extraction and speed estimation methods.
  • Validated estimates against Bluetooth sensor and Google traffic data.
Southeast University Jan. 2023 - Jun. 2023

Vessel Traffic Flow Modeling in Inland Waterways

Analyzed vessel traffic flow dynamics using multi-source maritime traffic datasets and developed a piecewise fundamental diagram model.

  • Investigated relationships among vessel speed, spacing, and traffic density.
  • Characterized traffic flow states in inland restricted waterways.
